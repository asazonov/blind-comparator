# Blind Comparator BC-10

A blind listening test for electric guitars. Ten instruments drawn at random from 275,
all their factory clips streamed from Thomann, five candidates each round — always from
the same maker, with Epiphone counted as Gibson and Squier as Fender, and each Harley
Benton or Larry Carlton grouped with whatever its model copies.

The report scores you against chance with an exact binomial test (guessing averages 2/10;
5/10 is where the result stops being explainable by luck) and totals how far your picks
sat above or below the price of the guitars actually playing.

Audio is streamed from Thomann's servers and is their copyright; nothing is redistributed
here. Needs an internet connection.
